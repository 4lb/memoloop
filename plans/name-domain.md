# Naming Research Summary — Language-Learning App

## Naming direction

The research focused on names that convey gradual learning, memory formation, repeated exposure and knowledge becoming part of everyday thinking.

The preferred naming style is:
- Natural English words rather than artificial startup-style names.
- Minimal, approachable and professional.
- Suggestive of learning without explicitly describing flashcards, reminders or language courses.
- Suitable for future expansion beyond Japanese.

## Final candidates

### 1. Suffuse

**Meaning:** To spread gradually throughout something or fill it completely.

Suffuse has an elegant, distinctive and slightly literary character. It captures the idea of knowledge gradually spreading through the mind through repeated exposure.

Preferred tagline: **"Let knowledge suffuse you."**

The phrase is grammatically correct and works as poetic brand language, although it is not everyday conversational English.

**Preferred domain: `suffuse.me`**

The combination creates an interesting expression: "Suffuse me." It feels personal, memorable and slightly poetic without being inherently erotic.

### 2. Leaven

**Meaning:** To introduce something that gradually changes, enriches or improves the whole.

Leaven originally refers to the substance that makes bread rise. Its figurative meaning is particularly appealing: a small influence can gradually transform something much larger.

For English speakers, it has warm, organic and positive associations. It is uncommon but recognisable, relatively easy to pronounce and spell, and has a pleasant sound reminiscent of "heaven".

Potential taglines:
- "A little learning goes a long way."
- "Small encounters. Lasting knowledge."
- "Let knowledge rise."

**Preferred domain direction: `leaven.*`**

Potential extensions to investigate include `.software`, `.study`, `.studio`, `.me` and `.one`. The `.eu` extension is not preferred.

## Other naming directions explored

Several alternatives were investigated and subsequently dropped, including Imbue, Instil, Engrave, Dropwise, Recollect, Imprint, Recall, Retain, Plum and various onomatopoeic names.

Many had existing commercial uses, particularly in language learning, flashcards, spaced repetition and educational software.

Suffuse and Leaven remain the most appealing candidates from the explored directions.

## Domain and trademark considerations

Domain availability has not yet been authoritatively verified. The suggested domain combinations must be checked through registrar searches and RDAP before registration.

Preliminary searches also identified existing software and company uses of both Suffuse and Leaven. These create potential search-identity and trademark concerns, but do not by themselves establish that either name is legally unavailable.

Neither candidate has received comprehensive trademark clearance. Before committing, both should undergo professional searches in the US, UK and EU, particularly in Nice Classes 9, 41 and 42.

The existing Polyhedron.one domain could also provide a parent-brand address, such as `suffuse.polyhedron.one` or `leaven.polyhedron.one`.

## Conclusion

The two strongest candidates are:

**Suffuse — `suffuse.me`**

The more distinctive, poetic and conceptually expressive option. Its domain construction is particularly appealing.

**Leaven — `leaven.*`**

The warmer, more approachable and naturally positive option. A suitable software- or education-oriented extension should be investigated.

Both names are worth carrying into the final naming and clearance stage. Domain availability and legal suitability remain unresolved.
